Our Breads, Pastries, and Cakes

Everything is baked by hand in small batches. Prices are listed as ranges because size and ingredients vary. There is no online checkout. To reserve an item, send us a pre-order request and we will confirm your pickup.

Breads

Sourdough Loaves

Tangy, chewy, and crusty, made with our house starter. Available in country white, whole wheat, and rye. Price range: $7 to $10.

Seeded and Multigrain Loaves

Hearty loaves topped with sunflower, pumpkin, and flax seeds. A favorite for sandwiches and toast. Price range: $8 to $11.

Rolls and Baguettes

Soft dinner rolls and crisp baguettes, sold by the half dozen or dozen. Price range: $4 to $12.

Pastries

Croissants and Danishes

Flaky, buttery layers baked golden each morning. Plain, almond, and chocolate options. Price range: $3 to $5 each.

Cinnamon Rolls and Morning Buns

Soft, spiced, and finished with a simple glaze. Price range: $4 to $6 each.

Cookies and Seasonal Tarts

Chocolate chip cookies and fruit tarts made with seasonal ingredients. Price range: $2 to $7 each.

Cakes

Everyday Cakes

Single-layer loaf cakes and round cakes in lemon, carrot, and chocolate. Price range: $18 to $35.

Custom Celebration Cakes

Layered cakes for birthdays, showers, and weddings, made to order. Please request at least one week ahead. Price range: $45 to $150, depending on size and design.

Planning an Event?

We prepare bread baskets, pastry platters, and dessert trays for meetings and parties. Tell us your date and guest count in the inquiry form and we will follow up with a quote.
