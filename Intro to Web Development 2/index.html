Welcome to North Star Bakery

Step inside and say hello. Every loaf, pastry, and cake at North Star Bakery is made by hand each morning for our neighbors, our commuters, and our families. Pre-order ahead for an easy pickup, or stop by and see what just came out of the oven.

What We're Known For

  • Slow-risen breads: Crusty sourdoughs and seeded loaves baked fresh every morning.
  • Handmade pastries: Buttery croissants, cinnamon rolls, and seasonal fruit tarts.
  • Celebration cakes: Custom cakes for birthdays, family gatherings, and community events.
  • Catering for events: Bread baskets and pastry platters for meetings, parties, and weddings.
