About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery started in a home kitchen with a jar of sourdough starter and a simple idea: a neighborhood should have a place where the bread is warm and everyone is greeted by name. Neighbors kept asking for more, and soon the home kitchen became a storefront on Maple Street.

Today we still bake in small batches, start before sunrise, and keep the same welcoming spirit we began with.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

Good bread starts with good ingredients. We choose them with care and keep the list short.

  • Local first: We buy flour, eggs, dairy, and seasonal fruit from regional farms and mills whenever we can.
  • Simple ingredients: Our breads are made with flour, water, salt, and time, with no artificial additives.
  • Community minded: We donate unsold bread at the end of each day to neighbors in need.

Meet the Team

Maria Lopez, Head Baker and Founder

Maria began baking at age 12 and has been shaping loaves for more than 15 years. She created our house sourdough starter and still mixes the first batch every morning.
